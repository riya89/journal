# 🌿 Raindrop Journal - User Manual

Welcome to your personal wellness companion! This guide will help you make the most of Raindrop Journal.

---

## 🏠 Getting Started

### Your Home Page
When you open Raindrop Journal, you'll see your monthly calendar. Each day is a card where you can:
- **Click any day** to write a journal entry
- **See colored cards** for days with entries
- **Track your mood** with a simple 1-5 scale

### Navigation Icons
Look for the floating icons on the right side of your screen:
- ⏳ **Hourglass** - Monthly Planner (manage your tasks)
- 🔮 **Crystal Ball** - AI Companion (chat for support)
- 🌙 **Moon** - Mood Dashboard (view your emotional journey)
- 🍄 **Mushroom** - Growth Garden (track achievements)
- 🏺 **Jar** - Gratitude Jar (collect grateful moments)
- ⏰ **Time Capsule** - Messages to your future self

---

## ✍️ Daily Journaling

### Writing an Entry
1. Click on today's date (or any past date)
2. A beautiful book-style modal opens
3. Add a title (optional)
4. Select your mood (1-5 scale)
5. Write your thoughts
6. Click "Save Entry"

### Tips for Journaling
- **Be honest** - This is your private space
- **Write regularly** - Build a streak!
- **Use templates** - Click "Templates" for prompts
- **Track word count** - See your progress at the bottom

### Streaks
- Journal daily to build your streak
- Missed a day? Use "Streak Recovery" (limited uses)
- Longer streaks unlock special badges!

---

## 📅 Monthly Planner

### Creating Tasks
1. Click the ⏳ Hourglass icon
2. Click "Add Task"
3. Choose a category (Work, Personal, Health, etc.)
4. Set if it's recurring or one-time
5. Save and watch your productivity grow!

### Task Categories
- 💼 **Work** - Professional tasks
- 👤 **Personal** - Personal goals
- 💪 **Health** - Wellness activities
- 👥 **Social** - Social commitments
- 📚 **Learning** - Educational pursuits
- 🎨 **Creative** - Creative projects
- 💰 **Finance** - Money matters
- 📌 **Other** - Everything else

### Dopamine Tracker
See your task completion visualized in a beautiful graph. Perfect days (all tasks done) are highlighted!

## 🎮 Growth Garden (Gamification)

### XP System
Earn experience points by:
- Writing journal entries (50 XP)
- Completing tasks (10 XP)
- Finishing quests (100-1000 XP)
- Maintaining streaks (bonus XP)

### Quests
**Daily Quests:**
- Write a journal entry
- Track your mood
- Complete 5 tasks

**Weekly Quests:**
- Journal 5 days this week
- Complete 20 tasks
- Maintain 7-day streak

**Monthly Quests:**
- Journal 20 days this month
- Write 10,000 words total
- Complete 100 tasks

### Badges
Unlock badges for:
- **Streak milestones** (7, 14, 21, 30, 60, 90 days)
- **Achievements** (first entry, 100 entries, etc.)
- **Special accomplishments** (perfect week, word master)

## ⏰ Time Capsule

### Creating a Capsule
1. Click the ⏰ Time Capsule icon
2. Click "Create New Capsule"
3. Write a message to your future self
4. List your current goals
5. Choose unlock date (30, 90, or 365 days)
6. Lock it away!

### Locked Capsules
- See countdown to unlock
- Can't open until the date arrives
- Builds anticipation and reflection

### Unlocked Capsules
- Read your past message
- Compare mood (then vs now)
- Check off achieved goals
- Celebrate your growth!

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Can't Save Entry?
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you're logged in
- Try refreshing the page

### Streak Not Updating?
- Entries must be saved before midnight
- Check if entry was actually saved
- Refresh the page to see updates

### AI Not Responding?
- Check internet connection
- Wait a moment and try again
- Refresh if issue persists

### Theme Not Switching?
- Click the theme toggle button
- Clear browser cache if needed
- Refresh the page

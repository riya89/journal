# Delete Recurring Tasks - User Guide

## Three Ways to Delete Recurring Tasks

### 1. Delete This Occurrence Only
**How:** Hover over a specific date cell and click the small ✕ button that appears next to the checkbox.

**What happens:** Only that specific date's occurrence is removed. The task continues to appear on all other applicable dates.

**Use case:** You're sick one day and can't do your workout, but want to keep it for all other days.

---

### 2. Delete All Occurrences in This Month
**How:** Click the ✕ button in the Actions column, then select "Delete all occurrences in this month".

**What happens:** All instances of this task are removed from the current month only. The task template remains and will still appear in other months.

**Use case:** You're traveling this month and can't maintain your routine, but want to resume it next month.

---

### 3. Delete All Occurrences
**How:** Click the ✕ button in the Actions column, then select "Delete all occurrences".

**What happens:** The entire recurring task template is deleted, removing it from all months (past, present, and future).

**Use case:** You're permanently stopping this habit or task.

---

## Tips

- **Hover over date cells** to see the delete button for single occurrences
- **Default selection** when deleting from Actions column is "Delete all occurrences in this month" (most common use case)
- **Can't undo** - be careful when selecting "Delete all occurrences"
- **Non-recurring tasks** don't show these options - they're just deleted immediately
